Why this plan is built this way ▸
  • Backward planning from test dates — Practice Tests, Term Exams & NRTs: topics finish 4 days before the test, then a self mock-test slot before you sit it. AIATS: everything is fully re-revised at least 10 days out, with 2 mock tests and ~200 practice questions per chapter worked into the days before.
  • Chapters broken into 3–7 topics — every Physics & Chemistry chapter is split into digestible sub-topics so no single day means "read a whole chapter."
  • Gentle, adapting load — the planner starts light and ramps up over the first few weeks, then auto-balances daily load against however much time is actually left before the exam it's prepping for.
  • Class 11 gets first claim on free days — whenever there's spare capacity (no near-term test topic due), the plan pulls in Class 11 chapters early, so the full 11th syllabus is wrapped up well before the AIATS-09 all-syllabus mocks begin (21 Mar 2027) — not crammed at the end.
  • Spaced repetition — once you check a topic off, light "quick revise" reminders resurface it at 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days later (the classic Ebbinghaus forgetting-curve spacing), rather than reading something once and never touching it again.
  • Active recall over re-reading — mock tests and per-chapter question practice are built into the plan itself, because testing yourself sticks better than re-reading notes.
  • Interleaving — each day mixes subjects (Physics/Chemistry/Botany/Zoology) rather than one giant single-subject block, which research on interleaved practice shows improves retention over "blocked" study.
